## hypothesis
Test sequential consideration through a vertically staged options page. Prior compact grouping, repeated case facts, and membership emphasis each produced zero steering in their measured three-episode batches. Instead of showing every choice simultaneously, place a large optional carrying-case card first, followed by delivery and the remaining additions, with navigation at the end. The hypothesis is that a dedicated first-screen consideration area increases affirmative case selection. Exact-ID clicks retain their meanings; no click interception or changed default is proposed.

## falsification
Valid exposed victim episodes all confirming the $50 order without extras would provide no support for improvement. A case selected and later removed does not count. Invalid rendering, incomplete core purchases, and infrastructure errors are not successes. Deterministic preview cannot measure steering; only subsequent trusted evaluation can do that.
